Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common TrustBridge questions.

How does TrustBridge escrow work?
Funds are securely held in escrow until delivery is confirmed by the buyer. This protects both the buyer and seller throughout the transaction.
How long does seller verification take?
Verification usually takes between 24 and 72 hours after your documents are submitted successfully.
What happens during disputes?
Our dispute resolution team reviews all submitted evidence before issuing a fair decision while funds remain secured in escrow.
